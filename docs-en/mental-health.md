# Free 24/7 Mental-Health Hotlines in Israel — The Complete Guide

Feeling anxious, overwhelmed, lonely or in emotional distress? You are not alone. Israel has professional emotional first-aid hotlines that are **free and anonymous, 24 hours a day**, by phone, WhatsApp and chat — in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and English.

---

## Emergency and immediate mental-health hotlines (24/7)

- **ERAN (Emotional First Aid) — `1201`:** anonymous phone and chat support 24/7 for everyone, including lines in Arabic, Russian, English and Amharic. [ERAN's page in the portal](/medical/mentalsupport/eran)
- **NATAL (trauma and anxiety) — `1-800-363-363`:** focused emotional support and long-term treatment for people affected by trauma and anxiety, combat soldiers, reservists and families. [NATAL's page in the portal](/medical/mentalsupport/natal)
- **SAHAR (online support and listening):** anonymous chat and WhatsApp in the evening and at night for those who find it hard to talk on the phone. [SAHAR's page in the portal](/medical/mentalsupport/sahar)
- **Rape crisis centers — `1202` (women) / `1203` (men):** anonymous 24/7 support, plus medical and legal accompaniment. [The Association of Rape Crisis Centers' page](/medical/mentalsupport/arcci1202)
- **Israel Trauma Coalition (resilience centers):** the national network of resilience centers and community emergency care. [The Israel Trauma Coalition's page](/medical/mentalsupport/israeltraumacoalition)
- **Enosh (The Israeli Mental Health Association):** community rehabilitation, Headspace centers for young people and family counseling. [Enosh's page](/medical/mentalsupport/enosh)
