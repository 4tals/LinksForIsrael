# Healthcare in Israel — Health Funds, Urgent Care and Your Rights

Every resident of Israel is covered by National Health Insurance and belongs to one of four health funds (kupot cholim). The basic basket of services is identical in all of them; they differ in clinic locations, doctor availability and supplementary plans. This guide collects the numbers and steps you need — for new olim and for anyone looking for a doctor.

---

## In an emergency

- **Life-threatening — Magen David Adom: `101`.** [MDA's page in the portal](/medical/main/magendavidadom)
- **Emergency room:** in an urgent case you can go to the ER of any general hospital, even without a referral. The visit carries a fee; you are exempt if you bring a doctor's letter or a payment commitment from your fund (Tofes 17), or if you are hospitalized. [Details from the Ministry of Health](https://www.gov.il/en/service/emergency-room-payment)
- **Emotional distress:** ERAN `1201`, 24/7 — [mental-health hotlines guide](/docs/mental-health?lang=en)

## The four health funds

- **Clalit:** `*2700` — [clalit.co.il](https://www.clalit.co.il)
- **Maccabi:** `*3555` — [maccabi4u.co.il](https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/en/)
- **Meuhedet:** `*3833` — [meuhedet.co.il](https://www.meuhedet.co.il/en/)
- **Leumit:** `*507` — [leumit.co.il](https://www.leumit.co.il/en/)

Through your fund's call center and app you book family-doctor and specialist appointments, get nurse advice, and find an urgent-care center when the clinic is closed. You can ask the fund for a doctor who speaks your language.

## New olim: registration and exemption

- **Where to register:** at Ben Gurion Airport, at the Ministry of Aliyah and Integration desk. If you did not register there — at any post office, or on the National Insurance website from three weeks after Aliyah. After 90 days, registration is done only at a National Insurance branch.
- **Exemption from health insurance contributions:** an oleh with no income is exempt for the first 6 months, and for up to 12 months while receiving a living allowance from the Ministry of Aliyah and Integration.
- [Registering with a health fund and paying contributions as an oleh — National Insurance (Hebrew)](https://www.btl.gov.il/ZcuyotAsdience/oleChdash/Pages/rishum.aspx)
- Help in your own language: [Yad L'Olim](/olim/main/yadlolim), [Nefesh B'Nefesh](/olim/main/nefeshbnefesh), and the [New Olim Guide](/docs/olim?lang=en).

## Switching funds

You can switch funds up to twice in 12 months: with the online form on the National Insurance website (free) or at a post office (for a fee). The switch takes effect on one of six fixed dates a year. [Switching health funds — National Insurance (Hebrew)](https://www.btl.gov.il/insurance/health_insurance/rishumkupot/pages/maaverkupot.aspx)

## Rights, questions and complaints

- **Kol HaBriut — Ministry of Health hotline: `*5400`** (or `08-6241010`). Sunday–Thursday 8:00–18:00, Friday 8:00–13:00, in Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, English, French and Amharic. It answers questions about your rights under the National Health Insurance Law and takes complaints about the health funds. [Hotline page](https://www.gov.il/en/service/kol-briut-moked)
