Whether you are an Israeli on relocation, a student at a university abroad, a traveler, or part of a Jewish community anywhere in the world — this guide gathers the most important verified links in one place, by country and need.

## 2. How to donate to an Israeli nonprofit with a tax deduction from abroad

- **United States (501(c)(3)):**
  - **[PEF Israel Endowment Funds](/abroad/diasporaandgiving/pefisrael)** — lets you give with a US tax deduction to any verified Israeli nonprofit holding a Proper Management certificate.
  - **[JGive](/economic/donations/jgive)** and **[IsraelGives](/abroad/diasporaandgiving/israelgives)** — digital platforms that issue recognized tax receipts.
  - Or filter directly by **US 501(c)(3)** in the English portal: **[/en?us501c3=1](/en?us501c3=1)**.
- **United Kingdom (Gift Aid), Canada (CRA) and Australia:**
  - **[IsraelGives](/abroad/diasporaandgiving/israelgives)** issues local tax receipts in more than 30 countries, including Gift Aid in the UK and CRA receipts in Canada.

## 3. Returning residents (_Toshav Chozer_) and Israeli communities abroad

Israelis who have lived abroad for more than two years are entitled to **returning resident** benefits (customs, tax, employment and returning scientists):

1. Check your eligibility in advance on the official portal: **[Returning Residents — Ministry of Aliyah and Integration (\*2994)](/abroad/israelisandconsular/toshavchozer)**.
2. Arrange continuous health coverage and redeem the waiting period with **[National Insurance (Bituach Leumi) — Israeli residents abroad](/abroad/israelisandconsular/bituachleumiabroad)**.
3. Scientists, physicians and academics abroad: join **[ScienceAbroad](/abroad/israelisandconsular/scienceabroad)** for support returning to academia and industry.
4. Israeli and Jewish communities abroad: **[IAC](/abroad/israelisandconsular/iac)**, **[Moishe House / Mem Global](/abroad/diasporaandgiving/moishehouse)**, **[PJ Library](/abroad/diasporaandgiving/pjlibrary)**, **[Masorti Olami](/judaismheritage/masortiandcommunities/masortiolami)** and **[Chabad.org](/judaismheritage/masortiandcommunities/chabad)**.
5. Moving savings back to Israel (regulated financial services): **[Money transfers to Israel](/olim/moneytransfers)**.
